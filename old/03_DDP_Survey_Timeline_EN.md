# A. Survey

## A-0. Design principles

| Principle | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 12 items / about 3 minutes. Short enough to complete standing, on the way out |
| **Timing** | **Strictly at exit, after the experience.** Announcing a survey on entry changes how people behave |
| **Key variable** | **Q5 (did you see the counter)** is the quasi-experimental grouping variable. That one item makes the comparison possible |
| **Collection** | QR → mobile web form (Google Forms or Tally) + 30 paper backups |
| **Target** | **150+ valid responses** (ideally 60+ in each Q5 group) |

# B. Measurement beyond the survey

## B-1. Automatic dwell log (Yellow Box)

The work is its own instrument. Data accumulates through the whole run with no staffing.

**Recorded:** sit time · stand time · duration (seconds)
**Stored:** SD card in the controller or local CSV. **No identifying information**
**Prep:** verify RTC sync before opening; back up after closing every day

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mean dwell per sitting | How well the work holds a person |
| Distribution of dwell times | Under 3 s = a quick check / over 30 s = actual staying. That threshold becomes a thesis metric |
| Sittings by day and hour | Correlation between crowding and interaction |
| **Re-sitting rate** | Sitting down again after seeing the counter = evidence of comprehension |

> **Re-sitting rate is likely to be the strongest finding of this exhibition.**
> Sitting again means someone understood the causal link and wanted to confirm it.

## B-2. Observation log (timed sampling)

Three times a day, 20 minutes each, spread across morning, afternoon and evening.

| Time | Visitor ID | Approach | Hesitation (s) | Action | Saw rear | Company | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | V-001 | Stool | 4 | Sat | Y | Solo | Looked back after standing |

- **Approach**: came within 1 m of the work
- **Hesitation**: seconds from approach to first contact. Longer means "am I allowed to touch this?" → a caption problem
- **Action**: sat / hung / touched / looked only
- **Saw rear**: Y if they walked behind the stool and looked at the counter
- **Company**: solo / pair / three or more — *whether solo visitors stay longer is an interesting question*

## B-3. Shot list

- [ ] Full sequence of both works (no visitors, frontal / oblique)
- [ ] Each work frontal — off and on, **matched angle pair**
- [ ] *Yellow Box* front lit + rear counter, **two frames at the same moment**
- [ ] Fade-out time-lapse (tripod, from sitting through standing to dark)
- [ ] Visitor interaction (only with written consent; prefer non-identifying angles)
- [ ] Installation process (for the thesis's fabrication chapter)

> ⚠️ Photographs showing visitors require **written consent.** Adding a "consent to photography"
> checkbox at the end of the survey form removes the need to collect it separately on site.

# C. Production timeline

**15 Sep 2026 (Tue) → assumed opening 3 Nov 2026 (Tue). Seven weeks.**

> ⚠️ **Chuseok 2026 falls on 24–26 September (Thu–Sat).** With the weekend, fabricators and
> suppliers stop for four days. **All external orders must be placed by Wednesday 23 September.**
> This is the single largest risk in the schedule.

### Phase 0 · 15–20 Sep — Concept lock & comprehensive exam (D-49 ~ D-44)
- [ ] Comprehensive examination (deck + script, at least two rehearsals)
- [ ] Fold panel feedback into the exhibition concept
- [x] Brand name confirmed — **Sai (사이)**
- [ ] Fix the installation sequence — threshold → dwelling

### Phase 1 · 21 Sep – 4 Oct — Design lock & ordering ⚠️ **critical path** (D-43 ~ D-30)
- [ ] **Confirm DDP regulations** — load-in times, lift dimensions, power capacity, floor loading, wall use, fire code
- [ ] Final drawings per work (dimensions, materials, finish)
- [ ] **All external orders placed by Wed 23 Sep**
- [ ] Order electronics (load sensing, occupancy sensing, controller, LEDs, numeric display, PSU, RTC, SD)
- [ ] Confirm budget; check grant and sponsorship deadlines

**Lead times (confirm with suppliers)**

| Item | Lead time | Order by |
|---|---|---|
| Steel frame fabrication & welding | 2–3 weeks | **23 Sep** |
| Frosted acrylic cutting | 1–2 weeks | **23 Sep** |
| Powder coating / finishing | 1 week after frames | 12 Oct |
| Electronics (domestic) | 3–7 days | 28 Sep |
| Electronics (overseas) | **3–4 weeks** | **18 Sep** ⚠️ |

### Phase 2 · 5–18 Oct — Fabrication & hardware (D-29 ~ D-16)
- [ ] Receive and inspect frames (dimensional errors must be caught now to be fixable)
- [ ] Move circuits from breadboard to PCB; test each work independently
- [ ] **Calibrate the *Yellow Box* fade timing** — verify the 1:1 ratio by measurement
- [ ] Finalise counter logic (count-up/down switching, digits, maximum value handling)
- [ ] **Implement and test dwell logging** — the foundation of the thesis data
- [ ] Tune the *Warm Welcome* load threshold (does a light bag register?)
- [ ] Tidy wiring and power so none of it is visible to visitors

### Phase 3 · 19–28 Oct — Integration & dry run (D-15 ~ D-6)
- [ ] **Dry-run installation in the studio** — stand both works in the real sequence
- [ ] **48-hour continuous run** — heat, light degradation, sensor drift, dropped log entries
- [ ] Third-party usability test (5–8 people who don't know the project) → basis for caption edits
- [ ] **Pilot the survey with 10 people** — do the items read, does it finish inside three minutes?
- [ ] Build and test the QR / mobile form; print 30 paper backups
- [ ] Finalise captions and wall text; send to print (Korean and English)
- [ ] Book transport, prepare packing materials
- [ ] Assemble a tools and spares kit (LEDs, sensors, cable, fuses, multimeter)

> **Do not skip this phase.** Standing the work up for the first time at the venue is
> the most common way a project like this fails.

### Phase 4 · 29 Oct – 2 Nov — Transport & install (D-5 ~ D-1)
- [ ] Pack, transport, load in at DDP (stick to the approved time slot)
- [ ] Install and connect power
- [ ] **Re-tune brightness under the venue's own lighting** — gallery lights kill the work's glow. This must happen on site
- [ ] Re-tune sensor thresholds on site (floor material and vibration change them)
- [ ] Mount captions; position the survey QR near the exit route
- [ ] **Shoot documentation before opening** — clean visitor-free frames exist only now
- [ ] Record the log start time; final RTC sync check

### Phase 5 · From 3 Nov — Run & data collection
**Daily**
- [ ] Check both works before opening
- [ ] **Back up the dwell log** after closing (SD → laptop → cloud, doubled)
- [ ] Track survey count against target

**Across the run**
- [ ] Observation log, 3 × 20 min per day
- [ ] Watch progress toward 150 surveys. If short, **move the QR signage** — it is usually a circulation problem
- [ ] Record 5–8 visitor interviews — language the survey cannot capture (thesis quotes come from here)
- [ ] Work through the shot list (B-3)

### Phase 6 · Two weeks after closing — Analysis
- [ ] Clean and code survey data (qualitative coding for open items Q7 and Q12)
- [ ] **Cross-tabulate by Q5** — Q6 accuracy and Q8 scores, counter group vs. non-counter group ← the key result
- [ ] Dwell log statistics (mean, distribution, re-sitting rate)
- [ ] Compare the alone × one-room subgroup against the whole sample
- [ ] Compile observation logs; transcribe interviews
- [ ] Draft the empirical chapter
- [ ] Update the portfolio; consider award submissions

## Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chuseok (24–26 Sep) delays fabrication** | High | Critical | Order by 23 Sep. Confirm supplier closures in writing beforehand |
| Overseas electronics shipping delay | Medium | Critical | Order by 18 Sep + **secure domestic substitutes in parallel** |
| DDP power or floor-load rules not met | Medium | Critical | Check first in Phase 1. The rules may change the design |
| Gallery lighting washes out the works | **High** | Major | Reserve on-site tuning time; negotiate lower ambient levels in advance |
| Low survey response rate | Medium | Major | Reposition the QR, staff the exit, consider a small thank-you gift |
| Sensor misfires / chatter | Medium | Major | Debouncing in firmware + spare sensors on hand |
| **Dwell log lost** | Low | **Critical** | Doubled daily backups. This data cannot be reproduced |
| Visitors don't touch anything | Medium | Major | State "Please sit" on the caption. Verify in the Phase 3 usability test |
